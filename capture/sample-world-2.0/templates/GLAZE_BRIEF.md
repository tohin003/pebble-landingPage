# The glaze brief (the ask card, S23: HS-G, or a real take RS-G)

For the lead and the take operator. The message goes into a fresh conversation named "Glaze", in no project.

## The message

> Scale the celadon glaze in Documents/Open day/glazes/celadon.csv to a 5 kg batch, and keep the tool for next time.

## Why it ends on the ask card (CHANGELOG 1.7.0, read only)

Pebble has to read the file before it can make the tool, so the tool is made after file output could shape it. 1.7.0
keeps a tool on its own only when nothing like that came first; any other keep asks: "Keep this tool in Pebble's
Toolbox?" with Discard and Keep, and Pebble says why it asks. That is a true, real route to the card, not a staged one.

## The right answer (the file's percentages, base to 100, iron as an addition)

| Material | Percent | Grams in a 5 kg batch |
|---|---|---|
| Potash feldspar | 25 | 1250 |
| Silica | 25 | 1250 |
| Whiting | 20 | 1000 |
| Kaolin | 20 | 1000 |
| Talc | 10 | 500 |
| Red iron oxide (addition) | 1 | 50 |

A take whose numbers differ is not used. (If Pebble counts the addition inside the 5 kg, the base comes to 4950 g; that
is a defensible reading, but the lead decides before the take is kept.)

## In frame

The ask card, its reason, Discard and Keep. The person's choice afterwards is out of the film (S23 ends on the card), so
choose Discard, which leaves the Toolbox empty for the next take.

## Reset between takes

Discard (or Remove) the tool; nothing else changes, because reading the file changes nothing.
